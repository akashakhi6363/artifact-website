ARTIFACT 03 / 05

Ashokan Pillar

"A Symbol of Dharma"

ORIGIN : India
PERIOD : Mauryan Empire
MATERIAL : Stone

The Ashokan pillars are among the finest examples of Mauryan craftsmanship and symbolize peace, governance and dharma.

ARTIFACT 04 / 05

Dancing Girl

"A Timeless Symbol of the Indus Valley"

ORIGIN : Mohenjo-daro
PERIOD : c. 2500 BCE
MATERIAL : Bronze

The Dancing Girl of Mohenjo-daro is one of the most remarkable artifacts of the Indus Valley Civilization. This small bronze sculpture is celebrated for its confident posture, intricate ornaments and exceptional ancient craftsmanship.

Shivaji Maharaj's Sword

"A Symbol of Courage"

ORIGIN : Maharashtra
PERIOD : 17th Century
MATERIAL : Steel

Associated with the Maratha legacy, the sword symbolizes courage, leadership and the spirit of resistance.
